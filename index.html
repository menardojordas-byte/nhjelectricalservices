About Us

Introduction

NHJ Electrical Services is a trusted provider of solar installation and electrical solutions, dedicated to delivering efficient, reliable, and sustainable energy systems for residential and commercial clients. Supported by a team of highly skilled professionals, we help homes and businesses enhance energy efficiency through expert installation, maintenance, and the application of innovative technologies.

We specialize in the design, installation, and maintenance of solar photovoltaic (PV) systems, alongside comprehensive electrical services ranging from residential wiring to large-scale industrial projects. With a strong emphasis on quality, safety, and customer satisfaction, we are committed to powering communities with dependable and eco-friendly energy solutions. Our company is focused on helping clients reduce energy costs while transitioning to clean, renewable energy—without compromising safety or performance. Every project we undertake is guided by strict quality standards, technical expertise, and a deep understanding of each client’s unique energy needs.

At NHJ Electrical Services, we believe in building long-term partnerships grounded in integrity, excellence, and innovation. By combining advanced solar technology with reliable electrical solutions, we empower homes and industries to achieve greater energy efficiency, sustainability, and continuous growth.

OUR VISION
To become a trusted leader in solar and electrical services, recognized for excellence, innovation, and commitment to clean and sustainable energy for homes and industries.
OUR MISSION
Our mission is to provide safe, high-quality electrical services and innovative solar energy systems tailored to our clients’ needs. We strive to build lasting relationships through integrity, professionalism, and dependable service while helping customers transition to a more sustainable future.

Government deductions, 13th month & pay frequency – editable

Gross = daily rate × days + overtime/premium pay + food allowance (per day × days) + bonus + 13th month pay. Net = gross − SSS − PhilHealth − Pag-IBIG − withholding tax − other deduction. Government contributions use the employee share on a monthly estimate (this period's basic + overtime pay × pay periods per month) and are split across the periods in a month. Withholding tax uses the TRAIN monthly table on that estimate after contributions; food allowance, bonus and 13th month pay are treated as non-taxable up to the ₱90,000 yearly exemption. 13th month pay = total basic salary earned in the year ÷ 12 (enter it per employee). Rates and tables are guides – verify with SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and BIR before paying.
